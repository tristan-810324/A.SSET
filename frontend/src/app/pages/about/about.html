About Project A.SSET

The campus runs better when every asset is accounted for.

Project A.SSET is an advanced system for school equipment tracking and hardware maintenance at Dr. Yanga's Colleges, Inc. It replaces scattered paperwork with one clear, reliable view of the devices, parts, loans, and repairs that support everyday learning.
